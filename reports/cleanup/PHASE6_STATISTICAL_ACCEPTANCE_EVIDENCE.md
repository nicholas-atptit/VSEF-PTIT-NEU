## Acceptance Policy Summary

Implemented default policy:
- require positive prediction effect size
- require economic metric delta not to deteriorate
- require cost-adjusted delta not to deteriorate when supplied
- reject excessive turnover penalty
- require bootstrap CI evidence for accepted status
- reject or downgrade unstable CI evidence
- require DM p-value below `0.05` for accepted status
- require sample size of at least `30`
- warn and downgrade for more than `20` unadjusted comparisons
- missing core evidence is never fabricated and cannot produce `accepted`

Statuses are limited to:
- `accepted`
- `inconclusive`
- `exploratory_only`
- `rejected`
