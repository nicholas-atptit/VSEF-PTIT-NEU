# Refactor Claim Boundary

This refactor changes code structure and reusable APIs only. It does not create
or alter empirical research results.

- offline diagnostic only
- no trading
- no profitability claim
- no BUY/SELL
- no recommendation or investment advice
- no live deployment
- no production
- no daily T+1 operation
- final rows scoring-only
- future-blind validation-only selection required
- no index-as-stock claim
- no VN100 assumption unless explicitly configured and locally available
- no champion replacement
