# Supervisor Review Checklist

## Scope and Governance

- [ ] Is v1 scope clearly frozen?
- [ ] Are excluded components clear?
- [ ] Are candidates clearly diagnostic-only?

## Experiment Evidence

- [ ] Are configs present?
- [ ] Are metrics and manifests referenced?
- [ ] Are baselines included?
- [ ] Are raw outputs not overclaimed?

## Forecasting Evidence

- [ ] Are baselines compared fairly?
- [ ] Are weak results reported honestly?
- [ ] Is multi-horizon evidence included?

## Risk-Aware Evidence

- [ ] Are forecast-only and risk-aware policies compared?
- [ ] Are drawdown, volatility, VaR/CVaR discussed?
- [ ] Is risk-aware limitation acknowledged?

## Regime Evidence

- [ ] Are regime rules explicit?
- [ ] Are thresholds documented?
- [ ] Are small-sample regimes flagged?
- [ ] Is no-universal-best-model thesis supported?

## Final Defense Readiness

- [ ] Is there a slide outline?
- [ ] Is there a defense script?
- [ ] Are limitations clear?
- [ ] Is future work realistic?
