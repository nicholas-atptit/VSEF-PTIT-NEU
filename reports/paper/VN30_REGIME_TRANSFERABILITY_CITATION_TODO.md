## Recommended Safe Literature-Review Wording

Efficient-market theory treats weak-form efficiency as a condition under which historical price and trading information should not provide persistent return predictability. Adaptive-market theory relaxes the idea of fixed efficiency by framing market efficiency and return predictability as evolving with market ecology, competition, learning, and changing conditions. Empirical AMH studies document time-varying return predictability in century-long DJIA evidence, in long-run US, UK, and Japanese equity-market evidence, and through calendar-effect evidence in China. Vietnam-specific AMH evidence has also been reported for HSX and HNX index data. These studies motivate a regime-dependent predictability framing, while the present paper tests a narrower VN30 stock-level regime-transferability design and does not infer causal regime-distance mechanisms.
